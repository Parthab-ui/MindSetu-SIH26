# Safety and responsible use

MindSetu is an SIH research prototype for personnel welfare support.

## It does not

- diagnose medical or psychological conditions;
- prescribe medication;
- replace emergency or professional services;
- make autonomous employment, disciplinary or welfare decisions.

## AI and model boundaries

Deterministic triage and research-model outputs are support signals, not diagnoses. Gemini is used for supportive communication and may fail or produce unusable output; the system validates responses and can fall back to deterministic supportive text.

## Crisis language

Messages containing identified self-harm or suicide language are routed through a safety-priority response path. This prototype does not replace local emergency procedures or qualified crisis services.

## Data

Use fictional or approved test data for demonstrations. Never commit API keys, passwords, sensitive personnel information or database dumps.
